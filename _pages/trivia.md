Praticed Sports
==========================================================================================

Practicing sports has helped me get out of burnouts numerous times. This takes effect on various aspects, including:

- improvements in the energy systems (especially fat oxidation) that could save glucose for the brain
- building a stronger neural drive generation ability in the Central Nervous System (CNS) that can be further used for better performing cognitive tasks
- focusing on personal objectives throughout a training session instead of paying too much attention to the comparison with peers, which also helps with keeping a clear and calm mindset during a sprint period of a scientific research project
- emphasis on fatigue management based on theories such as General Adaptation Syndrome (GAS), the importance of which is widely neglected by today's scientific research communities (especially the AI community)

The choice of sports can be crucial in terms of objectives. Here I share my choice of sports as well as the corresponding objectives.


- Skill-Based Sports
  - Motivation: acquiring useful motor skills for daily life, some of which can also be practiced throughout daily activities
  - Choices:
    - Motorcycle Enduro
      - Balance control (through standing stance)
      - Strength (through maneuvering drills)
    - Skiing
      - Balance control (through ski edge control)
      - Strength (anckle/knee-related muscle)
      - Calm mindset under pressure
    - Stand-Up Paddleboard (SUP)
      - Balance
      - Core strength
- Fitness
  - Motivation: building aerobic/anaerobic foundations for skill learning
  - Choices:
    - Cycling
      - Aerobic foundation (through FATmax riding before meals and threshold riding)
      - Anaerobic ability (through High-Intensity Interval Training and Repeated Sprint Training)
    - Running
      - Strength of leg and back (through plyometrics)
    - Strength Training
      - Strength of leg, back, and chest (through deadlift, bench press, rowing, and equipment training)
